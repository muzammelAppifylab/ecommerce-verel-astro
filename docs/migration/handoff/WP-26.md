## Differences from VAREL

- No `StaffInvite` table, token or `/accept-invite` page (D5): "Add staff" grants roles to an existing account
  or emails a sign-up link; the invitations list comes from the activity log. `staff.join` rows are gone.
- VAREL's role radio (Staff/Admin) + 8 area checkboxes is now one checkbox per template role; custom area
  mixes are made as roles in `/dashboard/roles`. "Choose at least one area" became "Choose at least one role with store access".
- Activity list is keyset ("50+ changes", LoadMore) instead of offset + total; the CSV is streamed.
- `lastActiveAt` from the activity log as in VAREL; ids are template `Int` user ids.
- The middleware writes `customer.delete` for `DELETE customers/:id` (R6 default: middleware only).
